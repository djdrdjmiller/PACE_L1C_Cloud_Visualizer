User clarified that operational cloudbow uses only the largest/best-covered angular branch; the other branch is irrelevant to this evaluation. Focus scientific comparison on the used branch, retaining the second only as an optional exploratory view. This clarification does not itself establish a source-view-to-operational-branch mapping for every bin, and unused-branch residuals should not be used to infer a normalization problem.

Bulk normalized phase matrices combine by scattering coefficient: P_mix=sum(beta_sca_i P_i)/sum(beta_sca_i), beta_sca_i=N_i times mean scattering cross section. If mode fractions describe geometric area, the corresponding weights are f_area_i times area-weighted mean Q_sca, normalized by their sum; multiplying area fractions by a per-particle C_sca would double-count size weighting. RFT literature describes area distributions, but HARP2's fraction metadata is not explicit. The user-requested literal mixture remains unchanged. The supplied qsca long_name says efficiency but its unit erroneously says µm²; numerical csca/qsca agrees with the gamma-distribution mean geometric area rather than pi*reff². That is evidence to investigate its efficiency convention, not authorization to silently change mixing weights. Exact common P-matrix normalization cannot be audited from this P21-only LUT.

GPC ATBD V1 (release 09/01/2026) downloaded to ignored cache and equations on pp. 5–6 visually inspected. Eq. 2 confirms normalized scattering-cross-section-weighted phase mixing; section 2.1.3 uses area distributions, without explicitly defining modal output fractions. The ATBD also confirms longest continuous cloudbow stretch is selected. A substantive background-basis discrepancy is recorded in RFT.md/provenance: Eq. 4 uses B*cos²+C, whereas the supplied reference uses B*(cos²−1)+C. Original coefficients and reference basis remain unchanged pending clarification; no operational reproduction claim is made. User may provide RFT papers later.
