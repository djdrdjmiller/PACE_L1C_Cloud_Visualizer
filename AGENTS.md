## Scientific invariants

- Measurements, spectra and selected bins use original OCI/HARP2 L1C sampling. Verify paired metadata and full geolocation; never manufacture correspondence by regridding science arrays. Display projection/cropping must preserve source identity and missing-data selection.
- Derive channels/sectors from wavelength/view metadata. Nominal angles select channels; actual bin/view geometry drives calculations. HARP2 maps share selected sources; never substitute another sector at a missing pixel.
- Keep TOA science separate from display enhancement and experimental corrected map fields. The user approved production Rayleigh correction over classified land and water after the offline trial. Use the same cached cloud/surface pressure and component subtraction; unclassified bins remain TOA, while unsupported classified corrections remain missing without TOA fallback. Legacy transmission remains water-only. `rayleigh_land=true` is the new default/browser policy; old saved Rayleigh recipes without that field restore water-only through `Recipe.from_dict` for compatibility. The browser explicitly migrates imports to the new land-enabled policy. Do not reintroduce a land bypass into current dashboard/deployment work. Spectra, angular observations, cloudbow/RFT comparisons and residuals remain TOA.
- Rayleigh removes modeled canonical I/Q/U components, with the established frame and irradiance conversions, before deriving magnitude/DoLP. The user authorized wavelength-dependent dry-air molecular depolarization on 2026-10-26: use the same Bates/Bodhaine King factor in phase elements and optical depth, retaining cached boundaries. Molecular anisotropy is distinct from multiple scattering, which remains excluded. See ATMOSPHERE.md for constants and unverified SWIR extrapolation. Do not subtract polarization magnitudes or apply the OCI scalar transmission LUT to Q/U. Describe this as experimental molecular single scattering, not validated full atmospheric correction.
- Surface transmission extrapolation to 1050 hPa was retired on 2026-10-19. The dashboard forces it off; recipe/API requests enabling it fail explicitly. Do not restore its control or relax rejection rules. The Rayleigh model's separate pressure support is unchanged.
- Cloudbow and RFT P12 reuse original same-bin stored C/B/A. As authorized 2026-10-26, RFT refl./component instead use optically thick single scattering, omega=1 and actual per-view geometry, with audited/declared phase normalization. The subsequent user-authorized four-order trial multiplies droplet components by 1+.5+.1+.01=1.61, leaving separate Rayleigh unchanged; weights are heuristic, not Cornet-derived or fitted. See docs/RFT.md. No coefficient refitting, new microphysical fitting, peak rescaling or phase-only display. Dashboard RFT uses user-confirmed number fractions times bulk Csca, normalized. Droplet contributions plus the separately displayed above-cloud Rayleigh path sum to RFT refl. when the existing cloud boundary supports it; no silent assumed pressure. Without a cache, curves are explicitly cloud-only. retained A/B/C mode responses count the B/C background once in their weighted average.
- Plot red hyperangular observations as discrete markers. Branch 1 is the comparison/residual branch; branch 2 is an observation sanity check. Do not claim the heuristic branch mapping proves operational selection. Preserve documented LUT/normalization/background-basis uncertainties.

- The user authorized the liquid-index workspace and operational/calculated left maps on 2026-10-27. Preserve the ATBD-style discrete default and explicit trapezoidal alternative, TOA samples, branch/quantity distinctions, 135° baseline reference and support/provenance in docs/LIQUID_INDEX.md. Do not tune/rescale the calculated LI to match GPC; operational parity is unverified.
